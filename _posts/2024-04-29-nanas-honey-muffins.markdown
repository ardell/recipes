Ingredients
-----------
- 2 cups all purpose flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 cup milk
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1/4 cup honey

Instructions
------------
1. Preheat oven to 400°. Line or grease muffin tin.

2. Mix together flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.

3. In a small bowl, mix together egg, milk, butter and honey. Stir into dry ingredients.

4. Fill muffin cups about 3/4 full.

5. Bake 15-18 minutes. Let cool in pan for 5 minutes.

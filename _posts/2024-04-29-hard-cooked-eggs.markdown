Ingredients
-----------
- eggs

Instructions
------------
1. Place eggs in saucepan large enough to hold them in single layer. Add cold
   water to cover eggs by 1 inch. Cover pan and heat on HIGH just to boiling.
   Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.

2. Remove pan from burner. Let eggs stand, covered, 15 minutes large eggs/20
   minutes extra-large eggs.

3. Drain immediately, peel, and serve warm, or cool completely under cold
   running water or in bowl of iced water, then refrigerate.

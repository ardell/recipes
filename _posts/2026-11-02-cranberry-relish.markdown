Ingredients
-----------
- 1 lb cranberries, washed
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tsp grated orange rind
- 1/2 cup orange juice

Makes 1.5 liters / 12 servings

Instructions
------------
1. Combine all ingredients in a large saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat until the cranberries pop open, about 10 minutes.
3. Skim the foam from the surface and discard.
4. Transfer to serving dish or jars for storage and cool.

Ingredients
-----------
- 1 bag Pepperidge Farm "herbed seasoned cubed" stuffing mix (the red one)
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 cup diced onion (sweet or yellow)
- 1 cup diced celery
- 2 cups diced baby bella mushrooms (optional)
- 2 cups chicken broth

Note: I often make a double recipe and use more butter, vegetables and broth
than called for, since (1) I like a lot of vegetables and that requires more
butter, and (2) double the amount of dry stuffing somehow needs more than
double the amount of liquid.

Instructions
------------
1. Heat butter in a large saucepan over medium-high heat.
   NOTE: If making a double batch, I use a LARGE stock pot or dutch oven.
2. Add vegetables and cook until tender-crisp.
3. Add broth and heat to a boil.
4. Remove saucepan from heat. Add stuffing and mix lightly. Allow stuffing to absorb liquid.
5. Heat oven to 350 degrees F.
6. Spoon mixture into a casserole dish (or disposable aluminum pan for easy clean-up!)
7. Cover with foil and bake for 35 minutes or until hot.
